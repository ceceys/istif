1.0.0 — 2026-11-05
- First release: plan truck, lorry and container loads in 3D; pieces drop into place in loading order and can be played step by step.
- Load rules: no stacking on top, floor only, top load limit, orientation (free, upright, fixed), delivery order; extra vehicles when the load does not fit, door allowance.
- Mixed fleet: when the main vehicle is full, the rest goes to the next vehicles; optionally the best vehicle for the remaining load is chosen. "Centre a short load" option.
- Preset types come from the vehicle catalogue (with source links); the Advanced tab lists container, semi-trailer, panel van and wagon records with search and maker filter.
- 3D view: zoom buttons and slider, double click to focus, save as PNG, full screen; weight along the length and axle shares.
- Manual editing: select and drag boxes in 3D, rotate with R/T/Y, push them to a wall; free space to the roof, sides and door is measured, faulty pieces turn red; changes can be undone and go into the file and the PDF.
- PDF loading plan, import from Excel and CSV, product and vehicle library, undo and redo, automatic draft every 30 seconds.
- Dark, light and system theme; metric and imperial units; interface in 12 languages: Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean.
